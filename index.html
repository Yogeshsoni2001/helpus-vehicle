GOOD QUESTIONS

Good to know.

Clear answers for a more comfortable drive.

Will people see my phone number?+

No. Your number stays private. The demo shows the intended experience; masked calling requires a configured telephony provider before launch.

Do scanners need to download an app?+

No app is needed to scan. A phone camera opens the vehicle's public contact page in a browser.

Can I use it for my bike or scooter?+

Yes. The concept is designed for cars, bikes, scooters, and other everyday vehicles.

Does HelpUs track my vehicle?+

This concept does not include live GPS tracking. Location sharing should only be added as an explicit, owner-controlled action.
